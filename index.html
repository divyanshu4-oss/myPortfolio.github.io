about

Built for the messy middle of the data stack

I work where raw, inconsistent data becomes something a business can act on. That means writing ETL pipelines in Python and PySpark, tuning SQL until reports stop timing out, and translating cloud pipeline output into dashboards non-technical stakeholders can read in ten seconds.

I'm AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate, with hands-on time in Azure Databricks and AWS, and I care as much about a pipeline's 3am failure mode as its happy path — 99.5% data availability wasn't an accident, it came from proactive monitoring and fast incident response.

Outside of production support and dashboards, I like taking a dataset nobody has looked at closely yet and finding the story in it.
